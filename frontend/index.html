Our Foundation

Built on Trust,
Grain by Grain

Manavta Agro Foods was established in 2016 at Tajoke Road, Rureke Kalan, District Barnala, Punjab. The mill is configured for parboiled rice processing with dedicated soaking, steaming and drying infrastructure.

Our facility is built around industrial parboiling workflows and commercial bulk supply. Buyer requirements can be aligned around rice variety, processing, broken percentage, polishing, moisture targets and packaging.

🎯
Our Mission
To process and supply consistent commercial rice lots against clear buyer specifications, with dependable mill operations and transparent communication.
🌟
Our Vision
To build long-term commercial relationships around dependable rice processing, practical quality control and buyer-specific supply.
💡
Our Values
Clear specifications, disciplined processing, practical quality control and buyer-focused service guide our work.
