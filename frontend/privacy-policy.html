Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Manavta Agro Foods handles information when you visit our website or contact us about rice products, commercial supply, quotations or samples.

Last updated: 10 October 2026Applies to: manavta-agro-foods.vercel.app and related website services

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Manavta Agro Foods, located at Tajoke Road, Rureke Kalan, District Barnala, Punjab, India, is responsible for the information collected through this website. For privacy questions or requests, email manavtaagrofood@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may process:

  • Enquiry details: name, business or organisation, email address, phone number, product interest, quantity, packaging or processing requirements, destination and message.
  • Technical information: information automatically provided by your browser or hosting/security services, such as IP address, device/browser details, request timestamps and diagnostic logs.
  • Account information: if account or administrative features are made available to you, authentication identifiers and session/security information needed to protect access.
  • Preferences: your saved cookie-consent selection and, if enabled by you, analytics-related information.

Please do not include passwords, payment card details, government identity numbers or other sensitive personal information in enquiry messages.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to product, quotation, sample and commercial supply enquiries.
  • Maintain enquiry records, follow up on requests and prevent duplicate submissions.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, secure and improve the website and its services.
  • Manage account access where account features are used.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and establish or respond to legitimate business claims.
  • Measure website usage only when optional analytics is configured and you have given consent through the cookie controls.

4. Legal grounds and choices

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information to respond to a request or take steps at your request, perform or prepare for a business relationship, meet legal obligations, protect our services, or on the basis of consent where consent is required. You may choose not to provide optional information, but we may be unable to respond to an enquiry without relevant contact and requirement details.

5. Service providers and sharing

Website information may be handled by service providers that help us operate the site, including Vercel for frontend hosting, Render for API hosting, MongoDB infrastructure for enquiry/account records, and Gmail SMTP for transactional email. If optional Google Analytics is configured and you opt in, Google may receive analytics data as described in its own documentation. These providers may process information on our behalf or under their own applicable terms.

We do not offer personal information for sale. We may disclose information where necessary to operate the service, comply with law, protect users or our business, or handle a business transfer.

6. International processing and security

Some service providers may process information in data centres outside your state or country. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Retention

We keep enquiry and related records for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain business records, address disputes, protect the service and meet applicable legal requirements. A fixed deletion schedule has not been established for every category. Cookie preferences remain in your browser until changed or cleared; enquiry drafts are held in session storage and are generally cleared when the browser session ends, unless the browser restores that session.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The website uses browser storage for essential functions, including temporarily preserving an enquiry draft and remembering your cookie choice. Authentication features may use a protected refresh-session cookie. Optional analytics is not loaded until you opt in, and only operates if an analytics measurement ID is configured. See our Cookie Policy for details and preference controls.

9. Your rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent for consent-based processing, or raise a concern about how information is handled. To make a request, email manavtaagrofood@gmail.com and describe the request. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

10. Children

This website is intended for business and commercial enquiries and is not designed to solicit information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, providers or legal obligations change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
