Cookie Policy

We aim to keep tracking to a minimum. Essential browser storage supports website functions; optional analytics is only loaded after you choose to allow it.

Last updated: 10 October 2026Applies to: this website and related services

1. What are cookies and browser storage?

Cookies are small pieces of data saved by a website in your browser. Websites can also use browser storage such as session storage and local storage. This policy describes these technologies together where relevant; not every item below is technically a cookie.

2. Technologies we may use

CategoryPurposeWhen used
Essential session storageTemporarily preserves enquiry form details and an idempotency reference so a request can be retried without unnecessary duplicate submissions.When you use the enquiry form; stored in the current browser session.
Consent preferenceRemembers whether you accepted optional analytics or chose essential storage only.Saved in local browser storage after you make a choice.
Authentication cookieSupports secure refresh sessions for protected account/admin features where those features are used. The refresh cookie is configured as HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and Secure in production.Only when using the related sign-in features.
Optional analyticsIf Google Analytics is configured, it helps measure aggregate website use and performance.Only after you opt in and only when an analytics measurement ID is configured.

We do not intentionally enable advertising or remarketing cookies in the current website configuration. If this changes, the policy and consent choices should be updated before those technologies are activated.

3. Your choices

When the consent notice appears, you can accept all categories, choose essential storage only, or customize your preferences. Essential storage supports the operation and security of the website and cannot be disabled through the preference panel. Optional analytics remains off unless you actively enable it.

To review or change your choice, use the Cookie Settings link in the website footer. If you have already dismissed the notice and cannot find that link, open the main website and use its footer. You can also clear site data through your browser settings; doing so may remove saved preferences and enquiry drafts.

4. Third-party services

If analytics is enabled by consent, Google Analytics scripts and related requests may be loaded from Google and may use their own cookies or identifiers. Google’s processing is also governed by its terms and privacy documentation. Hosting, database and email providers may independently use technical logs or security mechanisms needed to provide their services.

5. Retention

Session storage is normally cleared when the browser session ends, though some browsers may restore session data. The consent preference remains in local storage until you change it or clear site data. Authentication cookie duration is controlled by the relevant sign-in service configuration.

6. Browser controls

Most browsers let you block or remove cookies and clear site data. Blocking essential technologies may affect sign-in or other site functions. Browser-level controls do not necessarily remove all types of local storage automatically, so use the browser’s site-data settings if you want to clear stored website information.

7. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when website features or service providers change. For questions about cookies or privacy, contact manavtaagrofood@gmail.com. For more information about how enquiry and account data is handled, read our Privacy Policy.
